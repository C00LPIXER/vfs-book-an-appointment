After installing

  1. Android will ask to allow installing from wherever you opened the file. Allow it.
  2. Open the app, go to Settings (gear) → Permissions & background, and work down the list until every line has a tick. A line left crossed means the hourly check will be late, silent, or will not happen.
  3. On vivo, iQOO, Xiaomi, Oppo and Realme there is one more that Android cannot report: turn on Auto-start and allow high background power use in the phone's own battery settings. Without it those phones hold the alarm for hours — measured on an iQOO: six checks queued, the oldest five days late.

What it does

  • Checks once an hour, timed from when VFS itself last published — not from when you last opened the app.
  • When a slot appears: rings the caller tone on the alarm stream, vibrates, blinks the camera light, and puts an answer screen over the lock screen.
  • Keeps ringing every two minutes until somebody types a reply, so a pocket cannot silence it.
  • A slot still open at the next check says so quietly instead of ringing again.
  • Tells you when a check fails or VFS rate-limits the phone, rather than going quiet.
  • Keeps a history of every check and a log of the exact request and raw response.
  • Reads only the public feed — no account, no login, no booking.
